Privacy Policy

How Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd. safeguards your personal information, valuation data, and transaction confidentiality.

Home Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd., we understand that selling gold is a personal and financial decision. We are strictly committed to safeguarding your privacy and maintaining absolute confidentiality for all transactions conducted across our showrooms in Guwahati and Goalpara.

2. Information We Collect

When you consult our team, sell jewellery, or request assistance for pledged gold release, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Name, phone number, email address, and residential address.
  • Statutory Identity Proof (KYC): Copy of Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport) as mandated by Indian financial and police regulations.
  • Banking Information: Account number and IFSC code exclusively for initiating direct IMPS/RTGS payment disbursements.
  • Transaction Records: Details of gold items weighed, tested purity percentage, and payment vouchers.

3. How We Use & Protect Your Data

Your information is used solely for fulfilling your gold sale transaction, executing electronic bank payouts, and complying with statutory record-keeping requirements.

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or transaction details to any third-party marketing agencies.
  • All transaction documents and digital records are stored in secure, restricted-access corporate environments.
  • Disclosures are made strictly when required by law to authorized law enforcement or tax authorities under valid statutory processes.
