Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling old gold, live purity verification, and instant payments in Guwahati, Assam.

Where can I sell old gold in Guwahati for the highest value?
You can sell old gold at Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd. We operate two premier branches in Guwahati: Guwahati Club (Maa Kali Complex, GNB Road) and Adabari (Near NCS Square Mall). We evaluate gold using non-destructive German XRF technology, certified 3-decimal digital weighing scales, and live daily bullion rates with 0% melting loss, providing the highest cash or bank payout in Guwahati and Assam.
How is gold purity tested at Kalpana Gold Buyers?
Your gold purity is tested right in front of your eyes using certified German XRF spectrometer technology. The process takes less than 60 seconds and is 100% non-destructive—no melting, no acid scratching, and zero damage to your jewellery. You receive an accurate computerized karat reading (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K).
Can I release and sell pledged gold from banks or NBFCs in Guwahati?
Yes. Kalpana Gold Buyers specializes in releasing gold pledged with any bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank, Axis Bank) or NBFC (Muthoot Finance, Manappuram Finance, etc.) across Guwahati and Assam. We accompany you to the financial institution, clear the outstanding principal and interest dues, release the gold, evaluate it transparently, and hand over the surplus cash balance immediately.
What documents are required to sell gold in Assam?
In accordance with Indian statutory and AML guidelines, you only need to carry one valid Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) and your PAN card. While original purchase bills are welcome if available, they are not mandatory for selling old ancestral gold ornaments.
How quickly do I receive payment after valuation?
Payment is instantaneous. As soon as you accept the computerized valuation sheet, funds are transferred immediately to your bank account via IMPS/RTGS within 5 to 10 minutes, sent via UPI, or paid in cash in compliance with statutory regulations.
