Helpful Guidance

Tips for Your Branch Visit

Make your visit smooth and swift by reviewing these quick suggestions before coming in.

Bring Original Govt ID

Carry your physical or DigiLocker Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving License. A valid PAN card is required for transactions above statutory thresholds.

Have Bank Account Details

Keep your bank account number and IFSC code or cancelled cheque handy for instantaneous IMPS/RTGS transfers credited directly to your personal account.

Loan Pledging Papers (If Applicable)

If you wish to release gold pledged with a bank or NBFC, bring the original gold loan pledge receipt/ticket and loan statement so our executive can assist immediately.
