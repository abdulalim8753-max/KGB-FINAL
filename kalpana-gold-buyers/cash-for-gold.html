Answers to Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling old gold, purity verification, and payments in Guwahati.

How is the purity of my gold tested without damaging it?
Your gold is tested in front of your eyes using certified technology. Our computerized testing equipment measures the metallic composition and exact karat percentage (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K) without scratching the surface, cutting, or applying destructive acid.
Do I need the original purchase bill to sell my gold jewellery?
No, an original purchase invoice is not compulsory. Many old ancestral or gifted items do not have bills. All you need is a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) and your PAN card.
How does releasing pledged gold loans work?
Struggling with high compound interest on bank gold loans? We assist in settling your outstanding loan with any bank or any NBFC, release the ornaments, and pay you the balance surplus value immediately. You benefit from saving on mounting loan interest, receiving full bank visit accompaniment, and getting instant balance cash handed to you.
How soon do I receive payment after accepting the valuation?
Payment is disbursed instantly. We transfer funds immediately to your verified bank account via IMPS or RTGS within 5 to 10 minutes. Cash payments are also available within statutory RBI and Income Tax limits.
Do you deduct weight for stones, gems, or enamel?
Yes, because gold buyers only purchase pure metallic gold. If your jewellery has embedded synthetic stones, beads, or lacquer, we accurately calculate the stone weight so that you are evaluated strictly on the net gold weight. If desired, stones can be gently returned to you.
Can I sell broken gold ornaments or single earrings?
Absolutely. The market value of gold is determined exclusively by its net weight and karat purity, not its wearable condition. Broken chains, bent rings, and scrap gold receive the exact same high rate per gram as brand-new jewellery.
