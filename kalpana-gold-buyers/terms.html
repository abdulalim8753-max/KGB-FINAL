Terms & Conditions

Operational policies, gold appraisal standards, regulatory compliance, and customer safeguards at Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd.

Home Terms & Conditions

1. Overview & Eligibility

These Terms and Conditions govern all gold buying, evaluation, purity testing, and pledged gold settlement services provided by Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd. ("the Company") across its branches in Guwahati Club, Adabari, and Goalpara, Assam.

To sell gold ornaments, bullion, or coins, the customer must be an Indian citizen of at least 18 years of age and the rightful legal owner of the items offered.

2. Customer Identification (KYC Policy)

Under statutory Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards and local police regulations, every customer must provide:

  • One original Government-issued Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License).
  • Recent residential address verification and active mobile phone number.
  • A declaration confirming lawful ownership of the gold jewellery or ornaments.

Original purchase invoices are encouraged. If bills are unavailable, transactions proceed upon customer declaration and biometric/photo ID verification.

3. Non-Destructive Valuation & Purity Testing

All appraisals are conducted openly in front of the customer using certified German XRF (X-ray Fluorescence) spectrometers and 3-decimal precision digital scales.

  • We do not melt, scratch, or acid-damage your jewellery during appraisal.
  • Non-gold materials (semi-precious stones, wax, enamel, threads) are accurately deducted to isolate net gold weight.
  • Valuations reflect the prevailing live 24K/22K gold market rate in Guwahati/Assam at the time of testing.
  • You are under no obligation to sell. If you decide not to proceed, your gold is returned immediately in intact condition without any charges.

4. Release of Pledged Gold

For pledged gold loans from banks or NBFCs, the customer must present the original pledge receipt/pawn ticket. The company facilitates payment directly to the financial institution, accompanies the customer to safely receive the released ornaments, performs transparent XRF testing, and disburses the surplus cash balance immediately.

5. Payment Disbursement

Payment is disbursed immediately upon signing the sale confirmation voucher:

  • Direct Bank Transfer: Instant IMPS, RTGS, or NEFT directly into the customer's verified bank account (credited in under 5 minutes).
  • UPI: Direct instant mobile UPI transfer.
  • Cash: Permitted up to statutory limits prescribed by the Indian Income Tax Act.

6. Jurisdiction

Any disputes arising out of or in connection with transactions with Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd. shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Guwahati, Assam.
