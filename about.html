Our Story

Bringing Transparency to Gold Buying

Historically, individuals looking to liquidate old, broken, or unused gold in Assam were often faced with an opaque and intimidating process: unverified scratch tests, arbitrary melting losses of 5% to 15%, faulty scales, and undisclosed deductions.

Kalpana Gold Buyers Private Ltd. was established with a singular objective: to replace outdated practices with a modern, professional, and transparent gold evaluation service. Operating from two dedicated branches in Guwahati Club and Adabari, we provide customers with a safe, corporate environment where their gold is treated with the value and respect it deserves.

We do not rely on damaging acid scratches or forceful melting. Instead, your gold is tested in front of your eyes using certified technology and legal metrology certified digital scales, ensuring every milligram and karat is evaluated with 100% transparency.

Our Vision
